## Lesson → playbook edit

This is the 4th central-bank ladder graded under the modal-leg rule
(BanRep, RBI, CBR, RBNZ). It is the first where the modal leg **lost**,
and the first where the rule was read as a license to go above the mid.
Edit (appended to the modal-leg rule): the floor is the mid, and
**recording above the mid needs the same bar as going below it** (a
numeric multi-respondent survey, n >= 5). Also, when the central bank's
own last published track or a dated governor remark points away from
the modal leg, record the modal leg at the mid, not above it, even if
house calls have flipped since. One instance, so this is a cap on
direction only. No new edge claim.
