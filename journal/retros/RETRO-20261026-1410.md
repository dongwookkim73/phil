RETRO-20261005-0635) counted 2018, 2022 and 2026-R1 as n=3 right-ward misses. It told runoff
rows to apply a right-ward correction at FULL weight (~6pt on margin). The R2 rows used

on the final poll average and carry the historical error in the sd only. No directional shift.
