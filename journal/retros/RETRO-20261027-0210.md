## Lesson -> playbook edit

unless a dated source supports more.** One analogue upset (Vinicius 2024) is
not a method; this is the same principle as the Brazil "single analogue" ruling.
A verified tally or a verified denial of a leak would justify a larger
deviation. Without one, record near the mid as forecast-only. Evidence: the
Putellas and Kane rows above, net +0.124 vs the mid. Provisional, n=2.
