## AAA Oct24 bin: the estimate was wrong, and the method made it wrong

  shaded it to 0.55, and the print landed in the bin.
- Cause: the sd floor rule was written for bins whose book is a spread-out ladder

  said this ("possibly an early OPIS/station feed I lack") and I still sat 0.38

## Lesson -> playbook edit
AAA daily gas bins: when one bin's book is >= 0.80 before the print, record
est = mid. The sd-floor model applies only when no single bin holds >= 0.50
(a spread-out ladder). A concentrated pre-print book is information, not noise.
