### Lesson → playbook edit (tightening only)

The rule goes in as **say-the-word small-n shrink**. For `method=transcript-count(k/n)` or `method=market-series(k/n)` with n < 10, record `est = w*raw + (1-w)*mid` with `w = n/(n+20)`, and the note carries `raw=X w=Y`. This mirrors the postcount rule. The current-events riff words (geopolitics, AI) move with the news cycle, and the book sees that. A 3-rally count does not.

- Evidence: GA k/n rows 10, net +0.361, one rally. Below-side transcript-count flipped to +0.097 at n=15 over about 6 rallies.
- At n=3 the weight is w=0.13: Russia would have been 0.594, a dBrier of about +0.013 instead of +0.154.
- Provisional. Re-grade after 2 more rallies.
- It does not change any gate. It only shrinks the claimed edge, so it cannot create a bet the old rule would have blocked.
- Exempt: venue-local named-landmark/figure rows, which floor at the mid already.
