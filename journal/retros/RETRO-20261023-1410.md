## Lesson → edit

Tighten the rule's escape clause in `strategy/playbook.md`:
- A "numeric survey" means a multi-respondent poll with a published count,
  such as Reuters, Bloomberg or Interfax, n >= 5.
- A single analyst's probability split, a bank's house call, or a "consensus
  expects" sentence does not count.
- Below the mid with no qualifying survey, the modal leg is recorded at the
  mid, and its non-modal siblings sum to no more than 1 - mid.
